Act 0 · Foundation — 2026-07-16
Question · How to combine three signals honestly

Freezing CLIB as an offset

Adding a nucleotide-accessibility prior (CLIB) to semantic + grammar is easy; fitting its coefficient to the answer key is circular. We freeze CLIB at coefficient 1 and learn only b0, b1 — under honest nested CV.

Result: κ=1 is optimal, removes ~30% in-sample optimism, lifts a weak backbone's DMS 0.53→0.85. Four complexity axes all fail — an endpoint.

Question · CoVFit, ESM, Hie on one scale

The CoVFit × CAC benchmark

Scoring the SARS-CoV-2 spike with CoVFit, ESM2, and CSCS, then applying CAC (the CLIB three-pole). The domain-adapted backbone captures escape best.

Result: adding the CLIB pole sharply improves escape ranking; the CoVFit family leads.

Question · How to set b0, b1 without DMS

A label-free operating point — and the frequency proxy

Optimization needs a target, so we first define the bias-free fixed point (z-score then equal weight). Then we fit the coefficients to an escape-independent statistic — observed substitution frequency.

Result: frequency-fit b0≈0.19, b1≈1.5 (grammar + CLIB heavy). But lineage tasks leak (frequency ↔ escape overlap) — the honest tests are DMS and temporal.

Act II · Validating without labels
Question · How to judge performance without DMS

DMS-free evaluation — temporal, de-leaking, phylogeny

The future is genuinely held out. Fit only on pre-cutoff frequency and predict later emergence; re-evaluate with each variant's own mutations removed (LOVO); cross-check against Bloom phylogenetic fitness.

Result: emergence forecasting AUROC 0.899 (a tie with supervised); LOVO survives 5/5 — circularity refuted.

Question · Can label-free reach supervised · how far does it hold

Label-free synthesis — Bloom recovers the supervised weights

Coefficients fit to Bloom phylogenetic fitness (0.285, 0.942) recover the DMS-supervised weights (0.268, 0.955). The strong backbone transfers across strains (flagship). But it is backbone-dependent (general PLMs only).

Result: supervised-quality with zero escape labels; cross-strain protein-only 0.60→0.842; escape signal is from domain adaptation, not size.

Act III · The limits of the prior
Question · Does CLIB cross species

CLIB does not cross species

Extending to Influenza HA and HIV Env, CLIB is near-random. Not a codon-approximation artifact — confirmed with real CDS (WSN HA J02176, BG505 Env DQ208458).

Result: CLIB is SARS-specific (flu 0.50, HIV 0.58 vs SARS 0.883). The transferable component is the protein-LM signal, not CLIB.

Act IV · Point → Multi
Question · Why do old species fail · how to fix it

From single to multiple mutations — a formal extension

Hypothesis: escape in old, diverged species is combinatorial, not a single point mutation. We generalize each term to a sum over a mutation set M, and let evolutionary time t be the point↔multi knob.

Result (evidence): on Wu2020 combinatorial fitness the multi-CLIB↔fitness correlation rises with t (+0.206→+0.329). Multi-mutation semantic predicts H3N2 antigenic escape at AUROC 0.769 (vs 0.60 single-mutation).
