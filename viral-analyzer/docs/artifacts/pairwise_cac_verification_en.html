CLIMB · multi-mutation verification (cont.)

The complete pairwise CAC — and why codons don't matter here

The antigenic-distance test used semantic alone. We add the pairwise multi-CLIB term to make the complete CAC, and check it against a real-CDS codon table. The complete model wins — but the gain is genetic distance, not CLIB's mechanism, and real CDS equals the codon approximation.

Data Smith-2004 H3N2 antigenic map (273 strains) Backbone ESM2-650M Distance HI-titer antigenic units

What "complete pairwise CAC" means

Earlier, antigenic distance between two strains was predicted from semantic change only. The complete CAC adds the multi-CLIB of the whole mutation set separating the pair:

dist(A,B) = b0·semantic(A,B) + c·CLIB(A,B; t)
CLIB(A,B; t) = ½ Σi∈M [ log P(codon(aiA)→aiB, t) + log P(codon(aiB)→aiA, t) ]

M = the 328-position-aligned positions where A and B differ (dozens, for strains decades apart). Signed weights (b0, c) are fit; temporal confound is controlled as before (partial | year; within-year pairs).

Result — the complete CAC beats semantic-only

modelpartial | yearwithin-year (Δyr=0)
semantic only+0.289+0.508

After controlling for time, the complete CAC nearly doubles the semantic-only partial correlation (0.48 vs 0.29).

But the gain is genetic distance, not the mechanism At the strain-pair scale, pairwise CLIB is essentially a mutation count: corr(CLIB, Hamming) = 0.9989, and it is t-independent (identical from t=0.1 to 100). CAC[sem+Hamming] ≈ CAC[sem+CLIB] (partial 0.483 vs 0.481). So the improvement is genetic/mutational distance — which the PLM embedding under-weights after temporal control — not CLIB's nucleotide-accessibility mechanism. Over dozens of mutations, the accessibility microstructure washes out.

Real-CDS check — codon choice is irrelevant here

Because strain-pair CLIB is count-dominated, the specific codons don't matter — the modal approximation was fully justified. This contrasts with SARS-CoV-2 single-mutant escape, where a single position preserves the accessibility microstructure and codons did matter (yet even there real ≈ modal).

Takeaways

  • The complete CAC improves antigenic-distance prediction over semantic-only under temporal control (0.48 vs 0.29).
  • The source of the gain is genetic distance, which CLIB proxies perfectly at this scale — not CLIB's codon-accessibility mechanism.
  • Real CDS = modal codons here (identical result), because the signal is count-dominated.
  • Consistent with the broader finding: CLIB's mechanistic signal is specific to SARS-CoV-2 single mutants; for multi-mutation / cross-species, count and semantic lead.
